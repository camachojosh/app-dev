# My Favorite Series: **Breaking Bad**

![Breaking Bad](https://wallpaperaccess.com/full/2924772.jpg)

## 📺 About the Series
*Breaking Bad* is an American crime drama television series created by **Vince Gilligan**. The show aired for five seasons from 2008 to 2013 and is widely regarded as one of the greatest TV series of all time.

The story follows **Walter White**, a high school chemistry teacher turned methamphetamine producer, who partners with former student **Jesse Pinkman**. As Walter descends deeper into the criminal underworld, he adopts the alias **"Heisenberg"**.

> *"Chemistry is the study of transformation."* – Walter White

---

## 🌟Why I Love It
*Breaking Bad* tells the intense and emotional story of a high school chemistry teacher who turns to making meth to support his family. It’s a brilliant mix of:
- **Drama**
- **Crime**
- **Thriller**
- Intense storytelling and suspense
- Incredible character development
- Stunning cinematography
- Morally complex decisions and consequences
- Iconic lines and unforgettable scenes

---

## 👤Main Characters
1. **Walter White** – The brilliant but desperate protagonist.
2. **Jesse Pinkman** – Walter’s loyal and troubled partner.
3. **Skyler White** – Walter’s wife, caught between morality and survival.
4. **Hank Schrader** – DEA agent and Walter's brother-in-law.
5. **Saul Goodman** – A shady lawyer who later got his own spin-off.
6. **Gustavo Fring** – A calm, calculated, and terrifying drug lord.
